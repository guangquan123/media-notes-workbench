# Evidence contract

Use this order in the answer:

1. Give the direct conclusion from the current request and current evidence.
2. Add only materially relevant Obsidian support under
   `【来自我的知识库】`.
3. For each used hit, include:
   - `路径`: exact returned `path`
   - `标题`: exact returned `headingPath`
   - `依据`: a concise quote or faithful paraphrase of `excerpt`
4. Put unsupported professional advice under `通用建议` or label it as an
   inference.
5. Use `【知识库未找到明确依据】` only when the distinction matters to the
   request.

Do not list retrieved hits that did not influence the answer. Do not treat
retrieval score as factual confidence.
